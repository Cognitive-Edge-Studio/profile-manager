## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a bespoke editorial illustration for the right side of a freelance full-stack software developer's personal website hero. Create one square illustration, not a website screenshot. Subject: a quietly lived-in engineer's desk with an open graphite laptop, a slim second monitor showing abstract code lines and a small node graph, a warm ivory notebook with rough system sketches, a dark forest green coffee mug, and one small plant. No person. Style: sophisticated architectural pen-and-ink drawing with restrained gouache washes, fine imperfect dark ink lines, soft warm paper texture, subtle crosshatching, contemporary independent magazine editorial illustration. Composition: intimate three-quarter view, coherent perspective, comfortable margins, objects arranged naturally rather than floating. Warm off-white background #f5f2ea with muted sage green, graphite, and a very small dusty blue accent. Light comes from the upper left. The artwork should feel thoughtful, human, precise and understated, not shiny, not futuristic. Code on the monitors should look like understated short abstract strokes; no legible text, no invented company logos, no labels, no letters, no watermark. No neon, gradients, glowing circuitry, robot, 3D clay, glossy render, decorative sparkles, or generic AI imagery. Keep the upper background light and quiet so the illustration can sit inside a soft cream card.
