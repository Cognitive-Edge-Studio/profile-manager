# AI integration services

I help SaaS founders and small product teams add AI features to the software they already use. Give users a way to find answers in their documents, pull key details from uploaded files, or ask questions about business data in plain language.

I’m Shibly, an independent full-stack developer. I build the screens your users interact with and connect them to your backend, data, and AI model. You’ll work with me directly from scoping through launch.

## What I can add to your product

AI can help users find an answer, fill in a form from an uploaded document, or ask a question about their data inside your application. We can start with one feature and agree on what it needs to do before I build it.

### Generative AI & LLM integration

Add a language model where users already read or write. A support dashboard, for example, could summarize a long conversation so the next teammate can see what’s been tried and what still needs attention.

### RAG & intelligent search

Let users ask questions about help articles, manuals, or internal documents. The feature searches the material they’re allowed to see and can cite the passages it used so they can check the answer.

### Conversational AI integration

Give users a place to ask for help while they use your product. An assistant in a customer portal could explain which documents someone needs to submit and guide them to the right screen.

### Intelligent document processing

Pull specific details from PDFs, scans, and forms people upload. An invoice upload, for example, could prefill the supplier, invoice number, and total, with unclear values flagged for review.

### Natural-language data queries

Let people ask questions about business data they already have permission to access. A team member could ask, “How many orders shipped last week?” and see the answer in the dashboard they already use.

## How we’ll work together

We’ll agree on what a successful first release looks like and what your team will need to run the feature after launch. You’ll have a chance to try it with examples from your own application before it goes live.

### 1. Define the first feature

Tell me who will use it and what they need to do. I’ll review the relevant parts of your application and data, then outline the proposed work, timeline, and cost. If a technical question needs a prototype first, we’ll scope that separately.

### 2. Build it into your application

I’ll connect the feature to the screens, backend, and data it needs. We’ll decide who can use it, what information the model can see, and when a result should go to a person for review.

### 3. Test it with realistic examples

We’ll test against the results we agreed on. For document Q&A, that could mean checking whether answers cite the right sources and whether the feature tells users when it can’t find enough information.

### 4. Launch and hand it over

I’ll handle the deployment work we agreed on and show you how to run the feature. The handover covers the code, configuration, and account access included in our scope. We’ll also review expected model API and hosting costs.

## AI integration FAQs

Before you commit to an integration, you may want to know what needs to change in your app, what data the feature will use, and what it will cost to build and run. We’ll work through those details together as we scope the feature.

### Can you add AI to an application that’s already live?

Yes. I’ll review the relevant code, data sources, and access rules to see where the feature fits and what needs to change. We’ll agree on how to test and release it in your existing setup.

### What do you need from me to get started?

Tell me what your application does, who will use the feature, and give me an example of the result they’d expect. That helps me identify which parts of the code, APIs, or sample data I’ll need to review.

### How much does AI integration cost?

The build cost depends on the feature, the systems it connects to, the condition of the data, and the testing it needs. I’ll share a scoped estimate before development begins. Running costs such as model API calls, hosting, and storage are separate and depend on usage.

### What data will the AI provider receive?

That depends on the feature and the provider. Before building, we’ll identify which information needs to be sent, who should have access to it, and what restrictions apply. That helps us choose a setup that fits your product.

### Can you help after launch?

We can include post-launch support in the project scope. We’ll agree on what it covers, how long it runs, and who handles issues after handover.

## Tell me what you’d like your software to do

Tell me what your product does, who uses it, and what you’d like them to be able to do. A sample question, document, or screen can help me understand where the feature would fit and what a useful result would look like.
