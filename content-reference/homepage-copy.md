**Collaboration Process**

## How I will Work as Your Full-stack Developer

Each stage ends with a decision or a piece of work you can review.

### 1. Tell me where you're starting

Share the problem you want to solve, who the software is for, and what you already have. I'll ask about your priorities, budget, and preferred timeline so we can decide whether the project fits my skills and availability.

### 2. We'll agree on the work and how it fits together

You'll receive an outline of the work, what's included, the estimated timeline, and pricing. I'll explain what I need from you. If important questions remain, we'll agree on a paid assessment or small prototype before committing to the build.

### 3. I'll build it in stages, with you involved.

I'll show you progress at agreed review points and send a short weekly update. You can try the work and share feedback as it develops. If you want to add a feature, we'll agree on any changes to the cost and timeline before it joins the plan.

### 4. We'll check it, launch it, and hand it over.

I'll test the agreed features and give you time to review them before launch. For AI work, we'll check examples from its intended use. I'll handle the agreed deployment and hand over the code, documentation, and access included in our agreement.

**Maintenance is a service, not a leash.** If you'd like me to look after the software after launch, we can agree on support separately. You can also arrange a handover to your own team or another developer.

**FAQs**

## Have Questions?

A few practical questions about full-stack development, project fit, estimates, ownership, and ongoing costs. The details will depend on the work you're bringing.

### What is a full stack developer?

A full stack developer builds the screens people use and the logic and data behind them. Specialists focus on one area. Look for relevant projects, API skills, testing, deployment experience, and clear explanations. Handling connected changes together can reduce handoffs.

### How much will my project cost, and how long will it take?

Cost and timing depend on the features, integrations, existing code, technical unknowns, and review cycles. I'll assess the work and provide an estimated schedule and scoped price before development begins.

### Can you improve software I already have?

Yes, subject to reviewing it first. I'll look at the relevant code, setup, and requirements to understand what can be kept and what needs changing. That review helps us choose between an improvement, an integration, or a rebuild.

### Can one developer handle my project?

That depends on its size, deadline, and specialist requirements. I take on work I can deliver within my available capacity. If your project needs a larger team, I'll tell you before we commit or suggest a smaller first phase.

### Who owns the code, and which accounts will I need?

We'll record ownership, access, and handover arrangements in the project agreement. I'll identify the hosting and service accounts needed, including any third-party tools or licences, so you understand what belongs to you and what you pay for.

### What costs should I expect after the build?

Depending on the project, you may need to pay for hosting, domains, email, storage, or AI and other API usage. These are separate from development. I'll identify the likely services and their charging models when we plan the system.

### How will we know an AI feature works well enough?

We'll agree on representative tasks, expected behaviour, and acceptable limits before evaluating it. That can include answer quality, task completion, response time, and usage cost. The checks will reflect how people are actually meant to use it.
