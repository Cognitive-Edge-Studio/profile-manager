## What I can automate

### Lead Qualification & CRM Automation

When a website inquiry arrives, AI summarizes it and extracts details like the requested service and budget. The workflow updates the HubSpot contact and uses your qualification rules to assign a follow-up or flag the inquiry for review. Test both paths with an inquiry that meets your rules and one with missing details.

### Email & Inbox Automation

When a message arrives in a shared inbox, the workflow can label it, send it to the right teammate, or prepare a reply. For example, it can route a billing question to finance and draft an acknowledgment for the inbox owner to review and send. Your team can test it with sample messages and check the labels, routing, and drafts.

### Customer Support Workflow Automation

When a support ticket opens, the workflow can summarize the issue, apply your priority rules, and assign it to the right help desk queue. A support rep can review a suggested reply in the ticket, and cases such as refund requests can go to a designated reviewer. Test it with routine and urgent tickets to check queue assignment and escalation.

### Document Workflow Automation

When an invoice lands in a shared folder, the workflow can extract the vendor, invoice number, and amount and prepare an entry for your accounting system. The finance reviewer checks those fields against the original PDF before the entry is posted. You can test the workflow with a clean invoice and one with a missing or unreadable field.

### Content Workflow Automation

When a brief is marked ready, the workflow can create a draft in your content management system and assign it to an editor. The editor reviews the draft against the brief and approves it before it goes live. Your team can check that the draft, source materials, and review task are in the right place.

### Reporting & Summary Automation

On a schedule you choose, the workflow can pull figures from your CRM or spreadsheets, calculate the totals, and draft a short summary of what changed. A manager can check the draft against the source figures before sharing it. For a weekly sales report, you can verify that the deal counts match the CRM and that the report is ready on schedule.

## How I build and hand over your workflow

### Map one process

We identify what starts it, which tools it touches, what decisions it makes, and who handles exceptions.

### Build and test it

I connect the tools and test the workflow with representative inputs, including missing information, duplicate submissions, and cases that need approval.

### Check the result together

We agree on what success looks like and run the workflow against those checks. That might mean confirming that invoices reach the right reviewer or measuring how quickly leads get assigned.

### Hand it over

I document what starts the workflow, where to check its status, and what to do if a step fails. We agree on who will own the workflow and what support is needed after launch.

## AI workflow automation FAQs

### What is AI workflow automation?

AI workflow automation uses AI for specific steps in a repeatable business process. An event, such as a new email or an uploaded invoice, starts the workflow. AI can read the message, extract details from the document, or draft a summary. Your business rules determine what happens next, such as updating a record, assigning a task, or sending the item to someone for approval.

### Can you work with the tools I already use?

I start with your current tools and check what each one can read, update, or trigger. If a built-in connection falls short, I can check whether the tool’s API supports a custom integration.

### What happens when the workflow can’t finish a task?

We decide which cases need a person and where they should appear. A missing invoice number might go to finance for review. A failed CRM update can alert the workflow owner with enough detail to investigate.

### What affects the cost of a project?

The main factors are how many tools need to connect, how many paths the workflow needs to handle, and what approvals and testing are required. I can scope the build and identify any ongoing software or AI usage costs once I understand the process.
