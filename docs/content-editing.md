# Editing and reviewing your website

Open [the dashboard](http://127.0.0.1:3000/admin/). Imported content starts in draft. A public address returns Page not found until that page and site settings have been published; legacy JSON is never used as a fallback.

Use titles and headings without full stops, including numbered article headings (for example `1 Define the task`). Paragraph punctuation remains unchanged. Owner services links point to **Home → services**, while contact and inquiry calls to action point to the **Contact** page. The existing email and external reference links retain their own destinations.

The **Pages**, **Articles**, and **Services** lists show actions beneath each title: **View** opens its published address, **Preview** opens the saved draft, and **Edit** opens the editor. View is disabled when the record has no current published version. Administrators also see **Delete**, which asks for confirmation before permanently removing the record and retains the existing required-link protections. Opening or cancelling this confirmation does not change content.

Every article includes a table of contents built from its Heading 2 sections and Heading 3 subsections. Edit those headings in **Article content**, save, then Preview to see the updated links. Heading 4 stays within the article without crowding the contents. On desktop, the sticky reading rail keeps a compact TOC and the Hire me card visible together; only the TOC links scroll, and the panel shrinks for shorter windows. On phones/tablets, the expandable TOC stays sticky while reading and collapses after choosing a section. Section jumps leave room for the sticky header. Links highlight the current section, support keyboard focus, and respect reduced-motion preferences. It uses the existing heading anchors; changing a heading also changes its anchor, so update any manually authored links to that heading. No separate TOC fields or new pages are required.

External web links within articles automatically include `rel="nofollow"`, for existing and future content, in previews and published articles. This covers article text and article buttons/cards, while internal website links, section anchors, email and phone links retain their normal behavior. To edit a text link, select it in **Articles → Content → Article content**, open its link editor, and use **Advanced link type** (`nofollow` or `sponsored`). Article external links keep nofollow even if that field is empty; sponsored and new-tab protections are preserved. Article buttons use **Links → Link type**.

1. Open **Pages**, **Articles**, **Services**, or **Selected work** and choose a record.
2. Edit its Content fields. Home and About sections follow the order in Page sections. Services and Selected work cards come from their own collections, ordered by Display order. Keep existing layouts unless changing the design is intended.
3. Select text in the editor to change headings, emphasis, clickable text, or links. Use Heading 3 to start process steps, FAQ questions, benefits, and interests. Articles support Heading 2–4, lists, quotes, tables, links, and images.
4. Under **Links**, edit button text and choose a website page, external address, email, or phone destination. An optional section anchor points to a named section. Deleted/unpublished page references do not become public links. Related content selections control recommendations, including service pages. Select **Required for publishing** for a link essential to the page; header/footer navigation is always required.
5. Select **Save Draft**, then **Preview**. Preview shows saved drafts in the website design and keeps navigation within authenticated previews. Unsaved text is not included.
6. Open **Publishing** and use **Re-check saved draft**. Fix blocking errors; review suggestions such as title length or repeated descriptions. Warnings do not rewrite your text. An administrator can publish reviewed records; editors can save drafts. Make selected images Public first, then publish destination pages, then the records linking to them, then site settings. Private images remain available in authenticated previews.

Site settings controls profile/contact details, header/footer navigation, the header button, portraits, and contact-form wording. Its Preview button shows the homepage using saved draft settings. The Contact layout keeps introduction, next steps, form, and alternative-contact areas in their existing grid. Its form heading, supporting text, image, and extra buttons are editable. Form field behavior and validation remain code.

Under **SEO**, enter your search title/description and sharing image. Social title/description are optional overrides; omitted page descriptions/images use site defaults. The normal Canonical address is calculated from the page route; leave its override empty unless intentionally selecting another original address. A slug is the editable last part of a service/article URL. The Canonical address label is retained and SEO auto-generation buttons remain removed. **Hide from search** adds noindex and removes the page from the sitemap; it does not make the page private. The Publishing panel explains effective settings. Local previews/deployments stay noindex regardless of the checkbox. Production indexing requires developer configuration.

The generated Services pillar and nine service page drafts have been removed at your request. **Services** is empty for your own pages later. Imports will not recreate those pages. Edit the original homepage service cards under **Pages → Home → Page sections → Services → Content**: Heading 3 starts a category, Heading 4 starts a card, and the following text is its summary. The Services menu links to that homepage section. Empty service templates remain available for later owner-authored content; no new service page appears until you create it yourself.

Changing a published service/article slug and publishing creates an automatic permanent redirect from its previous address. Saving a draft does not change the public URL. Further renames redirect directly to the latest address. Administrators can add separate permanent/temporary redirects under Redirects; private/system paths, chains and collisions are rejected. Update required links in records listed by the Publishing panel before unpublishing or deleting a destination, removing its required section anchor, or making a referenced image private.

The finalized homepage and Contact forms save inquiries privately when enabled on a published site. Draft previews allow text/calculator review but disable submission. Only administrators can open **Inquiries**, mark new/read/replied, check notification status and retry a failed notification. Status changes do not send replies; original submitted content is read-only. Real notification email remains disabled until a sending service is configured. The supplied receiving address is private configuration and does not replace the displayed contact email. See [contact delivery](contact-delivery.md) for setup, retry and retention instructions.

Under **Site settings → Contact form**, edit labels/help, homepage form heading, message example, submit text and optional next-step note. On the Contact page, its form section heading takes priority over the global fallback. Required fields and the truthful saved-message confirmation remain fixed. Publishing settings applies the text on the next request. Contact errors retain visitor input; unchanged retries safely check the same inquiry. The current inline privacy notice uses a working local 180-day scheduled-retention policy that must be reviewed before launch. No new public pages were added. Calculator arithmetic, explanatory tool UI, decorative illustrations, and technology marks remain code assets.

Public requests read published records even when the visitor is signed in. Previews require an editor session on every request, stay noindex, and use private/no-store responses. No preview secrets are placed in links. Public pages, navigation, metadata and sitemap read fresh published content on the next request; reload an already-open tab. Use **Check published page / retry** in Publishing to check the actual HTTP response and title. It reports failures honestly; retry after correcting publication/settings or a temporary server failure. Its time is the last check in this browser session, not an indexing or ranking result. External sharing sites may retain older previews. Production CDN behavior still needs release verification in Slice 7.

Re-running the importer preserves later edits. Full original manuscripts and source-only form/booking instructions remain in private snapshots. Display fields contain the wording used in the existing design. Original article dates remain blank until a real date is supplied.

## Article tables on mobile

Keep the first column comfortably readable on mobile because it is visible before readers scroll across a table. Give labels sufficient width and wrap at word boundaries rather than splitting words mid-word. Use a 160px minimum as a starting point for ordinary article tables, review longer labels at phone widths, and widen further when needed. Keep horizontal scrolling inside the table rather than the whole page, and preserve any deliberately proportioned pricing or estimate columns.

This default applies to existing and future articles. Ordinary first columns use 30% of a table with a 160px minimum; pricing and estimate tables retain their existing proportions.
